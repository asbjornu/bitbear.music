[S3M], the ScreamTracker 3 Module format, was introduced with version 3.0 of
Sami "Psi" Tammilehto's [Scream Tracker], expanding on the Amiga [MOD] format
with up to 32 channels.

Scream Tracker was one of the first popular [tracker] programs on the [IBM
PC][pc] rather than the Amiga, and its interface went on to inspire later DOS
trackers such as [Impulse Tracker]. Various other trackers, including Impulse
Tracker itself, adopted support for the S3M format alongside their own native
ones.
