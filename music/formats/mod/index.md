[MOD], short for [module file][module-file], was the [tracker] format
pioneered on the [Commodore Amiga][amiga] by Karsten Obarski's
[Ultimate Soundtracker][soundtracker] and later popularized by [ProTracker].

A MOD file bundles up to four channels of digital samples together with the
pattern data describing which sample to play, at what pitch, and when –
combining the composition and its instruments into a single, self-contained
file. This is what made the format so influential: unlike sheet music or a
MIDI file, a MOD file sounds identical no matter whose computer plays it.
