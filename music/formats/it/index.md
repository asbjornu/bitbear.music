[IT], the [Impulse Tracker] format, is native to Jeffrey Lim's Impulse
Tracker, released in 1995 for [DOS].

Impulse Tracker's interface was heavily influenced by [Scream Tracker], but
its IT format introduced New Note Actions (NNAs), letting a composer decide
whether a new note on an already-playing channel should cut, continue, fade,
or release the note before it – a level of expressive control that many
[tracker] formats before it lacked. Impulse Tracker was released as
open-source software in 2014, twenty years after its debut.
