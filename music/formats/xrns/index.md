[`XRNS`][xrns] is the native song format of [Renoise], a digital audio
workstation built on the heritage of [tracker] software.

[DOS][dos] and [Amiga][amiga] tracker formats, `XRNS` is an
[XML][xml]-based archive bundling patterns, instruments, samples, and
effect chains into a single file, making it readable and editable by
third-party tools while still keeping Renoise's characteristic vertical
pattern-based sequencer at its core.
