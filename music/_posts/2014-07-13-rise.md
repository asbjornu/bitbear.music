"Rise" was Bitbear's entry to the [Solskogen 2014 Newschool Music
Competition][solskogen], where he ended up in 6th place, which he's
quite happy with considering how close he was to not submitting the
song at all.

<!--more-->

The intro of the song came about approximately six months before the
party while thinking about how lucky he was having a lovely daughter
and a loving and caring wife that lets him cultivate his passion for
music production and spend so much time on it. So the song is
basically a tribute to the two most important girls in his life.

The name is also drawn from his daughter entering his life and giving
**rise** to so many feelings he didn't know existed.
