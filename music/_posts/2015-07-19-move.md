Move! Bitbear's entry for the [Solskogen 2015 Newschool Music
Competition][solskogen].

<!--more-->

The first sketch of the song was started many years ago and then
placed in hibernation until the night of <time
datetime="2015-07-08">Thursday 8th of July 2015</time>, where Bitbear
finally had some time to attempt doing a track for the competition. He
brute forced himself through the night and produced an
arrangement-wise complete song that he delivered at the party the 10th
of July, but it was full of bugs so the mix sounded awful. Thus, he
reworked large parts of it so it sounds like this.
