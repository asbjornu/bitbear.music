"I Feel You" is Bitbear's contribution to [TRSAC 2025's Oldschool Music
Competition][oldschool-music-compo].

<!--more-->

The song was originally made in [Renoise][renoise] with lyrics and vocals
in mind, but as Bitbear has been struggling with insomnia, he focused on
exercise to exhaust his body such that he's able to sleep. This has given
much less time to make music, and the vocals are thus still incomplete.

Also, to Bitbear's surprise, [TRSAC][trsac] doesn't have a Streaming Music
competition this year. Thanks to his wife urging him to reproduce the song
in Impulse Tracker, he could happily submit it to the [Oldschool Music
Competition][oldschool-music-compo] instead. Producing in [Impulse
Tracker][impulse-tracker] (and [Schism Tracker][schism-tracker]) again
after not using it for over 25 years felt liberating, and he's definitely
going to spend more time producing oldskool tracker modules in the coming
months.

The idea for the song is that we all have hard days, and that everything
seems bleak and depressing – I feel you. Times are hard. However, we all
need to remind ourselves that we matter, we have value, and we are loved by
someone.

### Lyrics

> If you feel like you're worn out.
> Stretched thin, unable to make it through.
> I feel you.
>
> In constant doubt and frustration.
> Stressed out, 'cause everything's overdue.
> I feel you.
>
> Petrified and small.
> You crawl into a ball.
> It may feel like you have lost it all,
> But you are not alone.
> I will cradle your fall.
>
> Depressed and in isolation.
> Feels like everything's painted blue.
> I feel you.
>
> Can't speak, just wanting to scream out.
> A message hoping to reach you too:
> I feel you.
>
> We all have a role
> A purpose in it all
> Face your fears, break down your wall,
> You are not alone
> Rise and answer your call!
