"I Feel You" is Bitbear's contribution to [TRSAC 2025's Oldschool Music
Competition][trsac].

<!--more-->

The song was originally made in Renoise with lyrics and vocals in mind, but
as Bitbear has been struggling with insomnia, he focused on exercise to
exhaust his body such that he's able to sleep. This has given much less time
to make music, and the vocals are thus still incomplete.

Also, to Bitbear's surprise, TRSAC doesn't have a Streaming Music
competition this year. Thanks to his wife urging him to reproduce the song in
Impulse Tracker, he could happily submit it to the Oldskool Music
Competition instead. Producing in Impulse Tracker again after not using it
for over 25 years felt liberating, and he's definitely going to spend more
time producing oldskool tracker modules in the coming months.

The idea for the song is that we all have hard days, and that everything
seems bleak and depressing – I feel you. Times are hard. However, we all need
to remind ourselves that we matter, we have value, and we are loved by
someone.
