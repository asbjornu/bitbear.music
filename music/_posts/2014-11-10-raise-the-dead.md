"Raise The Dead" was Bitbear's entry to [Kindergarden 2014's Dance
Music Competition][kindergarden], where it placed 5th. Vocals from the
1955 Robert Aldrich movie ["Kiss me deadly"][kiss-me-deadly].

<!--more-->

### Lyrics

> *Scream*
>
> If you revive her, do you know what that would be? Resurrection,
> that's what it would be. And do you know what resurrection means? It
> means Raise The Dead. And just who do you think you are that you
> think you can Raise The Dead?
>
> It was kinda late. I had a long day. Winter — you know how it gets
> dark early. I had my lights on. I had them on. I know that.
>
> This guy come out of nowhere. Did he step out or run out? Or maybe
> he was pushed? Pushed. More like he was pushed!
>
> Raise. Raise. Raise. Raise. Raise. Raise. Raise The Dead.
