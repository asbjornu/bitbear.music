Bitbear's remix of [Lindstrøm][lindstrom]'s excellent
[Vōs-sākō-rv][vos-sako-rv], submitted to the [remix contest on
Beatport][beatport-remix-contest], made in its entirety with
[Renoise][renoise] 2.8.1.

<!--more-->

"Vōs-sākō-rv" is a reshuffle of the word "vossakorv" which is a
Norwegian sheep sausage ("korv") from the town of Voss. On
[NorwegianShop24.com][vossakorv], vossakorv is described as follows:

> Beef and mutton of the best quality, ground and kneaded in the
> traditional way. Naturally matured and smoked with a specially
> composed chip mixture with a recipe from the 19th century. It gives
> a great golden brown color and the characteristic taste that only
> genuine Vossakorv has. The recipe is secret, but the taste is for
> everyone.
