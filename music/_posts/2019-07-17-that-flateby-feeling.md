"That Flateby Feeling" was Bitbear's entry to the [Newschool Music
Competition][newschool-music] at [Solskogen 2019][solskogen], where it
placed 2nd. Cover art by [litn][]. <3

<!--more-->

After having spent over a year renovating most of the apartment,
changing position at work and delivering a talk at NDC Oslo, Bitbear
felt like he was coming out of a very stressful hibernation and
finally being awake and able to attend one of his favorite places on
earth: Solskogen. Having That Flateby Feeling again is awesome!

The song is made from 4 different parts, some of which are more than
20 years old. Being short on time between [delivering a talk at
NDC][ndc-talk] and completing a song for Solskogen 2019, using these
old scraps is what got him to the finish line in time.
