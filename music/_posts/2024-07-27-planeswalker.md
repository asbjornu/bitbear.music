Inspiring horn and vocal samples forced the Planeswalker into
existence. He now roams the realms of spacetime, crossing between the
dimensions of reality.

<!--more-->

Completed some time in 2021, the song was mixed down and used in the
demo [Chemistry][chemistry] by [Ninjadev][ninjadev], released at
[Black Valley 2024][black-valley-2024] where it ranked 2nd in the
[Newschool Demo Competition][newschool-demo-competition]. See the
[demo on YouTube][demo] or its [demozoo entry][demozoo] for more
details.
