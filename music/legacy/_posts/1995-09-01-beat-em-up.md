Another song published for completeness. I think it starts out okay, but once
the lead kicks in after around 35 seconds into the song, it turns into a hot,
annoying mess.

<!--more-->

I believe "Beat'em Up" contains the most enervating lead I have ever produced,
and I am rather ashamed of having spent time and energy creating something this
horrific. Even at just 2 minutes, it feels too long, annoying and monotonous.

Enjoy!
