A fast-paced trance tune completed in just a few days. The main theme was
inspired by [DJ Tiësto][tiesto] and other trance artists I listened to at
the time.

<!--more-->

The sound quality took a step up from earlier tunes, mostly due
to finding better 16-bit samples and gaining a better understanding of how
to manipulate and combine instruments.

As with all the other tunes on [Beyond Fantasy][beyond-fantasy], “{{
page.title }}” was created in [Impulse Tracker][impulse-tracker] on
[MS-DOS][ms-dos].
