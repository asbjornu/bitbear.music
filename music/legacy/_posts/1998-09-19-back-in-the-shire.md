A remix of [Modulo One][modulo-one]'s song of the same name. The original
had a slower, more pop-inspired arrangement, but I wanted to juice it up
and made this trance version instead, which became quite popular during my
school's 1999 "russetid".

<!--more-->

As with all the other tunes on [Beyond Fantasy][beyond-fantasy], “{{
page.title }}” was created in [Impulse Tracker][impulse-tracker] on
[MS-DOS][ms-dos].
