I have no recollection of how I got hold of samples from the 1978
animated film [The Lord of the Rings][lotr-1978], but they somehow ended
up on my hard drive, and long before the Peter Jackson movies were even
thought of, this song came to life.

<!--more-->

As with all the other tunes on [Beyond Fantasy][beyond-fantasy], “{{
page.title}}” was created in [Impulse Tracker][impulse-tracker] on
[MS-DOS][ms-dos].
