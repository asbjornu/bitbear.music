The second of a three-part tune series, starting with [Blood][blood] and
ending with [Tears][tears], all built on the same samples. This particular
"Edit" came after several iterations on the same theme and ended up a bit
shorter than the others.

<!--more-->

As with all the other tunes on [Beyond Fantasy][beyond-fantasy], “{{
page.title }}” was created in [Impulse Tracker][impulse-tracker] on
[MS-DOS][ms-dos].
