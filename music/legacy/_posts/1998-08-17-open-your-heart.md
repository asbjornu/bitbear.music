"Open Your Heart" isn't really a Power of Creation production. [Puma][puma]
started the song and never finished it, so I finished it off myself,
liking it enough to include it on [Beyond Fantasy][beyond-fantasy].

<!--more-->

The song's wealth of new, high-quality samples also went on to inspire
several of the tunes on [3rd Floor][3rd-floor]. As with all the other tunes
on [Beyond Fantasy][beyond-fantasy], “{{ page.title }}” was created in
[Impulse Tracker][impulse-tracker] on [MS-DOS][ms-dos].
