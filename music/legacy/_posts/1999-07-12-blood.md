The first of a three-part tune series, followed by [Sweat][sweat] and
[Tears][tears], all built on the same samples. A trancy house tune fit
for its time, though it sounds rather dated now.

<!--more-->

As with all the other tunes on [3rd Floor][3rd-floor], “{{ page.title }}”
was created in [Impulse Tracker][impulse-tracker] on [MS-DOS][ms-dos].
