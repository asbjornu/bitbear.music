The final part of a three-part tune series, starting with [Blood][blood]
and continuing through [Sweat][sweat], all built on the same samples. A
mellow trance tune with a stereotypical blippy lead that some might find
catchy.

<!--more-->

As with all the other tunes on [3rd Floor][3rd-floor], “{{ page.title }}”
was created in [Impulse Tracker][impulse-tracker] on [MS-DOS][ms-dos].
