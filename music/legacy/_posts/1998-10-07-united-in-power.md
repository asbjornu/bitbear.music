Grew from a nice sine wave and a heavy kick. From there I threw together
some chords and finally came up with a lead that can barely carry the song
to its end.

<!--more-->

As with all the songs on the album, this tune was created in [Impulse
Tracker][impulse-tracker] 2.14 on [MS-DOS][ms-dos], where samples above
8 bits were a luxury — so if you find the sound quality lacking, that's
the main cause.

[ms-dos]: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MS-DOS
