Made to impress a girlfriend of mine at the time, aptly named "Veronica".
The song was actually finished before I thought of using her name in it,
but young and foolish as I was, I figured it might impress her, so I sat
in my room and whispered her name into a microphone, then dropped it into
a few strategic spots in the mix.
