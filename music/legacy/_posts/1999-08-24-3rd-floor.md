"3rd Floor" is the second and final album Bitbear produced under his
[demoscene][] alter ego [PoC][poc], short for "Power of Creation", while a
member of the [Dupe][dupe] cracking group.

<!--more-->

The rather randomly chosen title has no deeper meaning. All 14 songs
were tracked in [Impulse Tracker][impulse-tracker], with most samples
sourced from sample compilations or lifted from songs by artists he loved
at the time.
