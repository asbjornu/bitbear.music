{:.jumbotron}
**Puma** is a Norwegian [demoscene] musician from Oslo and one of
[Bitbear][bitbear]'s earliest collaborators.

<!--more-->

Puma was also known under the handles **Fulgore**, **CHiMERA**,
**Ordeux**, **Uranus** and **adMiXTURE**, and was a member of the
groups [Dupe][dupe], [Digiton][digiton], [ULTiMATE][ultimate] and
[XeNoBlast][xenoblast].

Puma and Bitbear, both 14 years old at the time, produced "[Cool
Interpreter][cool-interpreter]" together in February 1995, when Puma
still went by "Fulgore" and Bitbear by "PCi". A few years later, Puma
also contributed to two tracks on Bitbear's [Beyond
Fantasy][beyond-fantasy] album: "[Open Your Heart][open-your-heart]",
which he started but never finished, and "[Rage][rage]", which Bitbear
built on the disharmony and sound effects initially created by Puma.

Puma's own productions, such as "Consumed World", "Glowing lava" and
"Endless sky", can be found on his [Demozoo profile][demozoo].
